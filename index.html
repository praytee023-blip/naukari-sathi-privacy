Privacy Policy for Naukri Saathi

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Naukri Saathi helps users find information about jobs, examination results, and study resources. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Personal Information

The app does not require users to create an account or log in. We do not ask users to provide personal information directly through the app.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Features such as saved jobs or study tracking may store information locally on your device. This information is not intentionally sent to our servers by these features.

3. Notifications and Analytics

As currently configured, the app does not send notifications and does not use analytics services.

4. Advertising

The current version of the app does not display advertisements. If advertising services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data collection and use.

5. Third-Party Websites

The app may provide links to external websites, including official recruitment and examination websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended to provide general educational and job-related information. If you believe information has been collected improperly, please contact the app publisher through its official distribution page.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the official page where Naukri Saathi is distributed.
